The Four Pillars

Complete Care System

01
☀️

Light

Light is the engine of photosynthesis. Understanding your light conditions — intensity, duration, and spectrum — is the single most transformative step in plant care.

  • Direct sun: 6+ hrs of unfiltered sunlight
  • Bright indirect: near south/east windows
  • Low light: tolerates 2–3 hrs of ambient light
  • Rotate pots every 2 weeks for even growth
Requirement
02
💧

Water

Overwatering kills more houseplants than any other cause. Learn to read your plant's thirst — not the calendar — with the finger test and soil moisture indicators.

  • Check soil 2 inches deep before watering
  • Use room-temperature, filtered water
  • Ensure pots have drainage holes
  • Reduce watering by 50% in winter months
Requirement
03
🪱

Soil & Nutrients

Soil is more than a medium — it's a living ecosystem. The right mix provides drainage, aeration, and a slow-release nutrient reservoir your plant draws from over months.

  • pH 6.0–7.0 suits most tropical houseplants
  • Fertilise every 4 weeks in growing season
  • Add perlite for drainage improvement
  • Flush soil quarterly to prevent salt buildup
Requirement
04
🌬️

Air & Humidity

Tropical plants crave humidity levels of 50–70%. Dry indoor air is a silent stressor, causing leaf tip browning, stunted growth, and increased pest vulnerability.

  • Group plants together to raise local humidity
  • Use a pebble tray with water beneath the pot
  • Mist leaves in the morning, not at night
  • Avoid cold draughts from windows and A/C

Moderate Concern

Overwatering / Root Rot

Yellow leaves are the #1 symptom of overwatering. When roots sit in soggy soil, they cannot absorb oxygen, causing them to rot. The plant signals distress through yellowing from the bottom up.

Treatment Steps

  1. Remove the plant from its pot and inspect the roots.
  2. Trim any black, mushy roots with sterilised scissors.
  3. Allow roots to air-dry for 2–4 hours before repotting.
  4. Repot in fresh, well-draining soil with added perlite.
  5. Wait until the top 2 inches are completely dry before watering.
Prevention: Always check soil moisture before watering. Use a moisture meter for precision.

Year-Round Rituals

Seasonal Care Calendar

🌱

Spring

Mar — May

  • Resume regular watering schedule
  • Begin monthly fertilising
  • Repot root-bound plants
  • Check for pests after winter dormancy
  • Propagate cuttings for new growth
☀️

Summer

Jun — Aug

  • Increase watering frequency
  • Shield from harsh afternoon sun
  • Mist daily in hot, dry conditions
  • Fertilise every 2–3 weeks
  • Move outdoor plants inside during heatwaves
🍂

Autumn

Sep — Nov

  • Reduce watering by 30%
  • Taper fertilising to every 6 weeks
  • Move plants away from cold windows
  • Prune dead leaves and stems
  • Add supplemental grow lights if needed
❄️

Winter

Dec — Feb

  • Water only when soil is completely dry
  • Pause fertilising entirely
  • Keep away from cold draughts
  • Maintain 18–22°C room temperature
  • Wipe leaves to maximise light absorption
